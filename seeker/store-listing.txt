Long description:
Mirmobe Sky is a night-sky viewer for a phone. Hold it up and the glass follows the compass, or drag the sky with a finger. Stars, planets, constellations, comets, and the International Space Station are drawn on the device.

Location is optional. Allow it and the glass is set to your sky. Decline it and pick a city. The tip jar on the glass charges nothing; the count stays on the phone.

This Android app is a window onto the Mirmobe Sky website. It is software for looking at the sky. It is not an investment, and it does not move money.
