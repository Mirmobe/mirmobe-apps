Privacy

Mirmobe Sky. This page covers the web app and the Solana Seeker shell that opens it.

No account

Sky has no sign-in and no account. There is nothing to register.

Location

If you allow it, the page asks the browser for your position with high accuracy turned off, then uses that latitude and longitude to draw the sky overhead. The fix can still be precise. If you decline, the page does not read your position and you can pick a city instead.

The latitude, longitude, and place name stay in local storage on your device, along with display options (which layers are on, the field of view, the lamp), a compass alignment, and a tip-jar count. That tip jar charges nothing. Mirmobe has no server that receives this data.

Compass and motion

While the glass is open, the page may read the device compass and motion sensors so the sky can follow the phone. Those readings are used in the moment and are not stored.

Sharing

A sky card is drawn on your device. If you choose a share button, the page opens X, WhatsApp, Facebook, Reddit, Telegram, or your SMS app with text you send. Those services have their own policies. Downloading the card saves a PNG on your device.

Hosting and fonts

The app is static files on GitHub Pages. Loading it is a normal request to GitHub. The page also requests typefaces from Google Fonts, which is a request to Google, not to Mirmobe.

The Seeker app

The Solana dApp Store build is a shell that opens this same site. It asks for the phone's location permission only so this page can request a position. It does not add an account, and it does not send the saved sky settings anywhere else. Declining location still opens the glass, and you can pick a city.

Children

Sky is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect data through an account.

Deleting data

Clear this site's data in the browser, or uninstall the Seeker app and clear its storage. That removes the saved place, display options, alignment, and tip-jar count. There is no account to delete.
