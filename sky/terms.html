Terms

These terms cover Mirmobe Sky on the web and in the Solana Seeker shell from the dApp Store.

What it is

Mirmobe Sky is a night-sky viewer. Point a phone at the sky, or drag the glass, to see stars, planets, constellations, comets, and the space station. It is software. It is not an investment, and it does not move money.

The glass

Positions are computed on your device for a view of the sky. They are a guide, not a survey instrument. Do not point optics at the Sun. You are responsible for where you stand and what you point at the sky.

The tip jar

The tip jar on the glass charges nothing. The count stays on your device. Nothing is billed and nothing is sent.

The Seeker shell

The Android app published for Solana Seeker opens this website. Links that leave the site open in the phone's browser. The shell is a window onto the page, and these terms apply to that page.

Your device

Location and compass access are optional. If you decline location, pick a city. See the privacy page for what stays on the device.
